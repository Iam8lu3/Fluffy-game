# Fluffy: Nine Lives, Nine Legends — Concept Art Bible

> Companion to `chapter1.js`. Use this as the source of truth when commissioning
> or generating art for chapters 2 → 9. Every Fantasy variant must reuse the
> EXACT same layout/geometry as its Reality counterpart — same hotspot
> positions, same horizon, same furniture placement — only the visual
> interpretation changes. This is the core mechanic.

## Global style rules

| Aspect | Spec |
|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | **3:2 landscape** (e.g. 1536×1024) — matches existing room renders |
| Framing | First-person camera at cat eye-level (~30 cm off the floor) |
| Edge fade | Subtle vignette in the corners; no hard borders |
| Lighting (Reality) | Warm domestic — late-morning sun OR evening lamps |
| Lighting (Fantasy) | Candle-gold key + emerald-green rim + violet shadows |
| Palette anchors | Deep midnight blue, royal purple, antique gold, blood-red accent, emerald jewel-pop |
| What MUST stay aligned | Floor line, door positions, hero objects (bed, sink, fridge, etc.), window frame |
| What CAN transform | Materials, ornament, lighting, sky, decoration density, posters→tapestries, plastic→bronze, etc. |
| **Forbidden** | Fluffy himself in the scene art (he's a separate sprite layered on top) |

## Tone rules
- Fantasy is **earnest**, not parody. Fluffy genuinely lives in this world. The art should be *beautiful*, not absurd.
- Reality is **warm and lived-in**, not depressing. A house that is loved by the people who live there.

## Chapter II — The Food Bowl Temple

**Setting (Reality):** the kitchen feeding corner. Two ceramic bowls on a vinyl mat by the cabinets, a tall kibble container, fridge edge visible, a calendar magnet on the fridge, a window above the sink letting in morning light.

**Setting (Fantasy):** a temple of offerings. The two bowls become sacred basins on a marble dais; the kibble container is a fluted columnar urn; the fridge becomes a vault-door of polished obsidian inscribed with cooling glyphs; the calendar becomes a liturgical wheel of feast days.

### Reality prompt
```
Wide kitchen-floor-level view from a cat's perspective. Soft morning light from
a window above a stainless sink. Two ceramic pet bowls (one for food, one for
water) on a folded blue-checkered cloth mat, pushed against the toe-kick of
warm-wood cabinets. To the right, a 4ft tall clear-acrylic kibble container
with a metal scoop hanging on the side. Fridge edge visible left, with a child's
drawing magneted to it. Tile floor with a faint chip in one tile. Worn but
loved. Soft warm palette: butter-yellow walls, cream tile, oak cabinet wood.
3:2 aspect ratio, painterly storybook style, ground-level cat-eye camera.
```

### Fantasy prompt
```
Wide low-angle view of a candlelit stone temple chamber, cat-eye level. Two
shallow basins of polished alabaster on a raised marble dais carved with paw
glyphs. A fluted columnar urn of porphyry to the right, brimming with gilded
seed-pearls. Obsidian vault-door to the left, inscribed with cooling glyphs
glowing faint cyan. A liturgical calendar-wheel of bronze hangs in the
shadowed alcove above. Stained-glass window above the dais throws emerald
and gold light across the floor. Same composition and floor line as the
kitchen reference. Dark premium palette: midnight blue stone, antique gold,
candle-warm highlights, deep purple shadows. 3:2 aspect ratio, painterly
storybook style, ground-level cat-eye camera.
```

### Key objects to render separately
- Sacred Bowl (close-up icon) — for inventory glyph
- Tribute Kibble (singular piece, glowing) — collectible
- Fridge-Vault Seal — interactable
- The Sacred Mat (cloth → woven offering rug)

## Chapter III — The Curtain Kingdom

**Setting (Reality):** the family-room window with floor-to-ceiling curtains, a window seat / radiator beneath, a houseplant on a side table, a low climbing-shelf the cat uses to reach the curtain rod, view of the suburban street outside.

**Setting (Fantasy):** the **Border Realm** between Fluffy's apartment kingdom and Queen LaSqueeka's domain. Curtains become enormous tapestry-walls. The climbing-shelf becomes a stone ascension. The view outside the window becomes the boundary mist with LaSqueeka's distant tree-spires visible through it.

**Queen LaSqueeka's first appearance:** this is the chapter she debuts. Her sprite (NOT in the scene art) will be placed on the upper-right climbing-shelf hotspot. Make sure that shelf is clearly visible and clean-edged so her silhouette reads.

### Reality prompt
```
Floor-level view of a sunlit family-room window. Floor-to-ceiling sheer linen
curtains, slightly parted, the morning sun casting striped shadows across the
hardwood. A cushioned window seat below the sill with a folded knit throw. A
narrow houseplant (monstera) on a side table to the right. To the left, a
short wooden climbing-shelf the cat uses to reach the curtain rod — three
ledges, each padded with a small mat. Through the window: a quiet suburban
street, a single tree, a parked car. Warm domestic palette. 3:2 aspect ratio,
ground-level cat-eye camera, painterly storybook style.
```

### Fantasy prompt
```
Floor-level view of the Border Realm — the threshold where two feline kingdoms
meet. Two enormous tapestry-curtains of midnight-blue velvet embroidered with
gold star-paws hang from above, slightly parted, light streaming between them.
A carved-stone bench below the sill of a great cathedral window. To the right,
a vine-wrapped pillar with a single glowing acorn-fruit (Queen LaSqueeka's
domain bleeds into the frame here). To the left, a stepped ascension of three
worn marble ledges leading up to the high window. Through the window: a misty
borderland with distant tree-spires of an adjacent kingdom on the horizon.
Dark premium palette, candle-gold and emerald accents. Same composition and
floor line as the family-room reference. 3:2 aspect ratio, ground-level
cat-eye camera, painterly storybook style.
```

### Special direction
- The **upper ledge of the climbing shelf** must be wide enough and clean enough to comfortably seat Queen LaSqueeka's sprite. Save ~15% of the upper-right canvas for her.
- Reality and Fantasy share **the exact same world** — no separate "LaSqueeka world."

### Key objects
- Border Tapestry (curtain swatch icon)
- Glowing Acorn-Fruit (LaSqueeka's gift / quest item)
- The Climbing Stones (Reality: padded ledges; Fantasy: marble steps)

## Chapter IV — The Laundry Labyrinth

**Setting (Reality):** a basement laundry room. Washer + dryer side by side, a folding table piled with clean clothes, a hamper, a basement window high on the wall, exposed pipes overhead, a single bulb.

**Setting (Fantasy):** an endless maze of **cloth tunnels** (clothes hanging from ropes form arched passages) and **spinning enchanted machines** (the washer/dryer become glowing arcane orreries). The basement window becomes a slit-window in a fortress wall.

### Reality prompt
```
Floor-level view of a basement laundry room. Front-loading washer and dryer in
white side by side, the washer mid-cycle (clothes blurred behind glass). A
wood folding table to the right, piled with folded towels and t-shirts. A
plastic hamper at the foot of the table. Bare concrete floor with a faded
braided rug. A small high-set basement window letting in a pale shaft of
afternoon light. Exposed pipes overhead, a single bare bulb hanging. Slight
musty cozy palette. 3:2 aspect ratio, ground-level cat-eye camera, painterly
storybook style.
```

### Fantasy prompt
```
Floor-level view of a vast subterranean labyrinth. Cloth tunnels formed by
arched canopies of hanging silk and linen banners, stretching into the
distance. Two enormous spinning enchanted orreries (where the washer and
dryer stood) — bronze armatures rotating slowly, runes glowing faint blue
through the porthole-windows, garments whirling within like trapped spirits.
A worktable of weathered oak piled with folded silk vestments. A wicker
basket-cauldron beside it. A narrow arrow-slit window high on the stone wall
casts a single shaft of sacred light. Dark premium palette, candle-gold and
deep purple shadows. Same composition and floor line as the basement
reference. 3:2 aspect ratio, ground-level cat-eye camera, painterly
storybook style.
```

### Key objects
- Sock of Singularity (collectible — the famous missing sock)
- The Hamper-Cauldron
- A glowing dryer-sheet (rune-tile)

## Chapter V — The Bathroom Ocean

**Setting (Reality):** an ordinary bathroom — bathtub, sink, toilet, a bath mat, a window with frosted glass, towels hanging.

**Setting (Fantasy):** a vast inland sea. The bathtub becomes a **tidal basin** with miniature waves. The sink is a **shore-cliff**. The toilet becomes the **maelstrom of the deep**. Tile floor becomes wave-polished porcelain shores.

### Reality prompt
```
Floor-level view of a clean modern bathroom. White ceramic tub to the left
with a thin film of soap residue, a small shampoo bottle on the rim. A
pedestal sink to the right with a chrome faucet, two folded hand-towels on
a rack beside it. White toilet in the back-center with the lid down. A
striped blue-and-white bath mat. Frosted-glass window above the tub letting
in cool diffuse light. Hexagonal tile floor. Soft cool palette. 3:2 aspect
ratio, ground-level cat-eye camera, painterly storybook style.
```

### Fantasy prompt
```
Floor-level view of a vast porcelain seascape. The bathtub is now an inland
tidal basin — actual gentle waves lapping its rim, foam catching the light.
The pedestal sink is a sheer shore-cliff, water cascading from its mouth
into the sea. In the back, a great maelstrom — the toilet, transformed into
a spiral whirlpool with pale luminous depths. A wave-polished hexagonal
shore of pearl-tile. A frosted lantern-window high above the basin throws
cool aqua light. Subtle salt-spray in the air. Dark premium palette: deep
teal, pearl-white, candle-warm rim light. Same composition as the bathroom
reference. 3:2 aspect ratio, ground-level cat-eye camera, painterly
storybook style.
```

### Key objects
- Drowned Soap Bottle (sacred ampoule)
- Towel-Sail (a hanging towel, collectible)
- The Whirlpool Coin (item dropped near the toilet base)

**Setting (Fantasy):** a great open gathering place — rival feline kingdoms' camp-pavilions, banners, wandering adventurer-cats around a central brazier. The fence becomes a kingdom-wall. The trash cans become bronze offering-jars.

### Reality prompt
```
Floor-level view of a suburban back alley at dusk. Wooden fence stretching
left-to-right with a slight gap. A wooden back-stoop on the right with a
worn welcome mat. A blue recycling bin and two metal trash cans to the
left. A power pole in mid-distance, wires crossing the soft pink-orange
sky. Cracked asphalt with a single dandelion poking through. Two cat
silhouettes barely visible on the fence-top. Warm dusk palette. 3:2 aspect
ratio, ground-level cat-eye camera, painterly storybook style.
```

### Fantasy prompt
```
Floor-level view of a great twilight gathering ground beneath a star-pricked
sky. A high fortified kingdom-wall of weathered timbers stretching left to
right with a single ornate gate. To the right, the worn marble steps of an
embassy. To the left, three bronze offering-jars (where the trash cans
stood), one tipped slightly. A great central brazier with crackling
violet-gold flame. Camp-pavilions in mid-distance flying banners with
different feline house-sigils. A standing-stone where the power pole was, a
crow-totem perched atop. Several rival-kingdom envoys' silhouettes on the
wall-top. Dark premium palette: indigo sky, candle-gold flame, banner-
crimson and emerald. Same composition as the alley reference. 3:2 aspect

```

### Key objects

**Setting (Fantasy):** Fluffy's **highest royal seat** — the chimney is a turret of black stone, the satellite dish a bronze sun-disk, the lawn chair a carved throne, the clothesline a flag-line of kingdom banners.

### Reality prompt
```
Floor-level view from a flat apartment rooftop at golden hour. A brick

parapet wall in the foreground, beyond which a city skyline at sunset —
silhouettes of buildings, a few lit windows. Tar-paper roof surface with
a few weathered cracks. Warm golden-hour palette. 3:2 aspect ratio,
ground-level cat-eye camera, painterly storybook style.

Floor-level view from a high palace tower-top at gloaming. A turret of

### Reality prompt
```
Floor-level view from atop a couch cushion, looking down. A human (mid-30s,
gentle expression, soft sweater, jeans) kneeling on the rug a couple of feet
away, holding a small orange pill bottle in one hand and a soft pill-pocket
treat in the other. A white bath towel draped over their forearm.  A small
side table to the right with a glass of water and a phone. Warm domestic
lamp-light. The couch upholstery, the rug pattern, the wall art — all
cozy. 3:2 aspect ratio, slight downward camera tilt, painterly storybook
style.

Floor-level view from atop a great judgement-bench of polished wood, looking

crystalline reliquary in their right hand and a glowing tribute-fruit in

## Chapter IX — The Cosmic Window

**Setting (Reality):** the bedroom window at night. Curtains parted. Moonlit street, neighbor's porch light, a single tree. Star pinpricks in the sky. A book left open on the windowsill.

**Setting (Fantasy):** the **gateway to the stars**. The window becomes a great arched portal of starlight. The street becomes a celestial road. The tree becomes a sky-rooted World-Tree. The neighbor's porch becomes a distant kingdom-beacon. The book becomes a scriptural codex.

### Reality prompt
```
Floor-level view of a bedroom window at deep night. Curtains drawn aside,
the window glass clear. Beyond: a quiet residential street washed in
moonlight, the neighbor's porch lamp glowing soft yellow in the
middle-distance, a single oak tree in the foreground silhouetted against
the night sky. The sky itself dotted with stars and a single crescent moon
slightly off-center. On the wide windowsill: an open hardcover book pages
faintly riffled by a draft, a half-burned candle in a small holder, a
folded note. Soft cool moonlit palette with one warm point of light (the
porch lamp). 3:2 aspect ratio, ground-level cat-eye camera, painterly
storybook style.
```

### Fantasy prompt
```
Floor-level view of a vast arched portal at the edge of the world. The
threshold is wrought of pale stone inlaid with constellations. Beyond:
a celestial road of star-dust winding away into the deep, the distant
World-Tree (where the oak stood) crowned with constellations in its
branches, and a distant kingdom-beacon glowing warm gold (where the
neighbor's porch was). The sky is a deep ink-blue ocean of stars, a great
crescent moon hanging close enough to almost touch. On the wide window-
threshold: an open scriptural codex with letters of gold-fire, a half-spent
ritual candle in a brass holder, a sealed wax-stamped scroll. Same
composition as the bedroom window reference. Dark premium palette: ink-blue
cosmos, gold constellations, candle-warm threshold-light. 3:2 aspect
ratio, ground-level cat-eye camera, painterly storybook style.
```

### Key objects
- The Star-Codex (book)

## A few extra targets to commission

When the chapter rooms are done, the next priority assets are:

1. **Queen LaSqueeka sprite set** — same poses as Fluffy (idle, walk-1, walk-2, sit, leap, peek, glide, royal-pose) on a transparent background, square canvas. She should move *differently* — more upward / horizontal arcs since she owns vertical space.
2. **A unified inventory glyph set** — each chapter introduces ~3-5 collectibles. Small square (256×256) transparent PNG icons. Same illustrator style for visual consistency.
3. **Achievement / trial seals** — for the future achievements system. Heraldic shields (one per chapter completed). Same style as the existing chapter-card frames.
4. **Chapter title cards** — 1920×1080 hero illustrations for each chapter's intro screen. Same vibe as the existing main-menu reality artwork.

## Naming convention recommendation
When you upload, please name files like:
```
ch02-foodbowl-fantasy.png        ← room scene
ch02-foodbowl-reality.png
ch02-obj-sacredbowl.png          ← key object
fluffy-reality-idle.png          ← sprite
fluffy-fantasy-walk1.png
lasqueeka-fantasy-glide.png
```

It makes wiring them into the code dramatically faster.
