# Fluffy: Nine Lives, Nine Legends

Welcome to the strange little world of Fluffy.

This is a passion project being built one chapter at a time. Fluffy is a black cat living an ordinary life in an ordinary home... at least that's what humans think. From Fluffy's perspective, the apartment is a vast kingdom filled with ancient relics, hidden realms, mysterious powers, and legendary adventures.

The game is a story-driven point-and-click adventure where players can experience both sides of Fluffy's world:

**Reality** — the world as it truly is.

**Fantasy** — the world as Fluffy understands it.

Explore rooms, uncover secrets, collect relics, meet unusual characters, unlock achievements, and slowly piece together the legends of a cat who may be far more important than he appears.

Among those legends is the esteemed **Queen LaSqueeka, Sovereign of the Glider Dominion**, ruler of the Sky-Court of the Thousand Acorns and one of Fluffy's most distinguished allies.

This project is being created by someone who genuinely loves the idea and is figuring out many parts of game development along the way. Some systems are polished, some are experimental, and many things will continue to evolve over time.
